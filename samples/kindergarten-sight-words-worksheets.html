Playing with the Ball

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

The cat is on the mat. The dog is in the yard. The cat sees a red ball. The dog says, “Go see the ball!” The cat and the dog run to the ball. They play and laugh. It is sunny.

  1. Who is on the mat?
  2. Where is the dog?
  3. What color is the ball?
  4. What does the dog say?
  5. Who runs to the ball?
  6. What do the cat and the dog do after they get the ball?
  7. What is the main idea of the passage?
  8. How do you know the day is nice?
  9. Which two sight words appear in the sentence “The cat and the dog run to the ball”?
  10. Draw a picture of the cat and the dog playing with the ball.

Answer Key

  1. The cat is on the mat.
  2. The dog is in the yard.
  3. The ball is red.
  4. The dog says, “Go see the ball!”
  5. The cat and the dog run to the ball.
  6. They play and laugh.
  7. The passage is about the cat and dog finding a ball and playing together.
  8. Because the passage says, “It is sunny.”
  9. The sight words are “the” and “and.”
  10. (Student draws a picture.)
