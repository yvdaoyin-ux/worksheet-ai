A Day at the Community Garden

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

Sam loved exploring. One Saturday, his class took a field trip to the Sunnyville Community Garden. The garden had bright red tomatoes, tall green beans, and sweet orange carrots. Sam helped water the seedlings and learned how bees move from flower to flower, spreading pollen. He saw a ladybug climbing a leaf and a robin building a nest in a nearby tree. After the work, the teacher let the students taste a fresh strawberry. Sam said the strawberry was the juiciest he had ever eaten. He felt proud because he helped the garden grow.

  1. Who went to the Sunnyville Community Garden?
  2. What kinds of vegetables did the garden have?
  3. What is the main idea of the passage?
  4. What did Sam help do with the plants?
  5. Why do you think Sam felt proud at the end?
  6. How do you know the garden had insects living there?
  7. What caused the strawberry to be sweet and fresh?
  8. Put these events in the order they happened: (a) Sam tasted a strawberry, (b) the class visited the garden, (c) Sam helped water seedlings.
  9. Why did the teacher let the students taste a strawberry after the work?
  10. Have you ever helped plant or care for something outside? Write one sentence about it.

Answer Key

  1. Sam and his class went to the garden. (The passage says “his class took a field trip to the Sunnyville Community Garden.”)
  2. The garden had bright red tomatoes, tall green beans, and sweet orange carrots. (Listed directly in the second sentence.)
  3. The passage is about Sam’s experience helping and learning at a community garden.
  4. Sam helped water the seedlings. (The passage states “Sam helped water the seedlings.”)
  5. He felt proud because he helped the garden grow. (The passage says “He felt proud because he helped the garden grow.”)
  6. The passage mentions “a ladybug climbing a leaf and a robin building a nest,” showing insects and birds were present.
  7. The teacher gave them a fresh strawberry after the work, so it was fresh and sweet. (The passage says “After the work, the teacher let the students taste a fresh strawberry.”)
  8. Correct order: (b) the class visited the garden → (c) Sam helped water seedlings → (a) Sam tasted a strawberry.
  9. The teacher let them taste a strawberry after the work. (The passage says “After the work, the teacher let the students taste a fresh strawberry.”)
  10. Answers will vary; accept a sentence that describes a personal gardening or caring experience.
