# WorksheetAI 推广文案（复制即用）

网站：https://worksheet-ai-l1td.vercel.app

**核心原则**：先讲"我自己遇到的问题"，给免费价值，再问反馈。**别硬广**，Reddit/群普遍反感上来就贴链接。

---

## 1. Reddit — r/homeschool（主战场）

**标题（二选一）：**
- `I built a free tool that turns any topic into a printable worksheet with an answer key`
- `Made a free worksheet generator for my own kids — sharing in case it helps yours`

**正文：**
```
I kept digging through free printable sites for my kids and got tired of it, so I
built a small tool: type a topic (e.g. "grade 2 fractions") and it writes a clean,
printable worksheet + answer key in about 30 seconds. Works for K–5, in math,
reading and spelling.

It's free to try (2 worksheets a day, no signup): https://worksheet-ai-l1td.vercel.app

I'd love feedback from other homeschool parents — which grades/subjects do you
need most? I'll prioritize those.
```
> 发完**守在评论区**回复问题；可以顺手把大家要的主题做进工具（真实互动）。

## 3. Pinterest（这个人群超活跃）

做几张**练习纸截图**当图钉（用你生成的真实 worksheet 截图最好看）。图钉标题+描述：

- `Free Printable Worksheets in 30 Seconds` — `Type any topic → get a grade-appropriate worksheet with an answer key. Perfect for homeschool K–5. #homeschool #printables #worksheets`
- `2nd Grade Fractions Worksheets (Free & Printable)` — `Instant printable fractions worksheets with answer keys for 2nd grade. #2ndgrade #math #homeschool`
- `Kindergarten Sight Words Worksheets` — `Free printable sight word worksheets with answer keys. #kindergarten #sightwords #homeschool`
- `3rd Grade Multiplication Worksheets` — `Printable multiplication practice with answers in seconds. #3rdgrade #math #homeschool`

> Pinterest 每张图都链回对应页面，比如 `.../worksheets/2nd-grade-fractions-worksheets/`。
