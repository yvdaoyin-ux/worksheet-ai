  1. Firefighter – definition matches a person who puts out fires.
  2. Police officer – definition matches a person who protects the community.
  3. Librarian – definition matches a person who helps find books.
  4. Doctor – definition matches a person who treats illnesses.
  5. Teacher – definition matches a person who helps students learn.
  6. Farmer – definition matches a person who grows crops and raises animals.
  7. Engineer – definition matches a person who designs and builds things.
  8. Mail carrier – definition matches a person who delivers mail.
