3rd Grade Fractions Worksheet

Solve each problem. Show your work where needed.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. Write the fraction that represents one quarter.
  2. What is the equivalent fraction of \frac{1}{2} with denominator 4?
  3. Which is larger, \frac{3}{5} or \frac{3}{8}?
  4. Which is smaller, \frac{2}{7} or \frac{5}{7}?
  5. Fill in the missing numerator: \frac{?}{6} = \frac{3}{9}.
  6. Mark the point that shows \frac{1}{2} on the number line.
  7. Maya has 12 apples. She puts the same number of apples into each of 4 bags. What fraction of the apples are in one bag?
  8. Sam has a chocolate bar divided into 5 equal pieces. He gives 2 pieces to his friend. What fraction of the bar did Sam give away?
  9. What fraction of the bar is shaded?
  10. Solve the addition problem.

Answer Key

  1. \frac{1}{4} — one quarter is one part of four equal parts.
  2. \frac{2}{4} — multiply numerator and denominator of \frac{1}{2} by 2.
  3. \frac{3}{5} is larger — same numerator, larger denominator makes a smaller fraction, so \frac{3}{5} > \frac{3}{8}.
  4. \frac{2}{7} is smaller — same denominator, smaller numerator gives the smaller fraction.
  5. 2 — \frac{3}{9} simplifies to \frac{1}{3}; to get denominator 6 multiply numerator and denominator by 2, giving \frac{2}{6}.
  6. \frac{1}{2} — the point halfway between 0 and 1 on the number line represents one half.
  7. \frac{1}{4} — each bag gets 3 apples; 3/12 = 1/4 of the total.
  8. \frac{2}{5} — two of the five equal pieces are given away.
  9. \frac{3}{4} — three of the four equal sections are shaded.
  10. 395 — add ones (7+8=15, write 5 carry 1), tens (3+5+1=9), hundreds (2+1=3).
