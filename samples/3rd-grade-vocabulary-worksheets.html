Vocabulary: 3rd Grade

Use the word bank to complete each activity.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

predict habitat fraction neighbor journey measure respect breathe

A. Match each word to its meaning. B. Fill in the blank. C. Write your own sentence for two words.

  1. A place where plants and animals live.
  2. To say what will probably happen.
  3. One part of a whole, shown as a number over another number.
  4. A person who lives near you.
  5. A trip from one place to another, often long.
  6. To find out the size, length, or amount of something.
  7. To show admiration or consideration for someone.
  8. To take air into your lungs and let it out.
  9. During the science experiment, we had to ______ the temperature of the water.
  10. My ______ always helps me water my garden.
  11. If we ______ the ending of the story, we can guess what happens next.
  12. Animals in the forest have a special ______ that gives them food and shelter.
  13. We will ______ a long ______ across the state for the school fair.
  14. Write a sentence using "respect".
  15. Write a sentence using "journey".

Answer Key

  1. habitat – the place where plants and animals live.
  2. predict – to say what will probably happen.
  3. fraction – a part of a whole expressed as one number over another.
  4. neighbor – a person who lives near you.
  5. journey – a trip from one place to another.
  6. measure – to find out size, length, or amount.
  7. respect – showing admiration or consideration.
  8. breathe – to take air in and let it out.
  9. measure – we needed to measure the temperature.
  10. neighbor – my neighbor helps me water my garden.
  11. predict – we can predict the ending of the story.
  12. habitat – animals have a special habitat for food and shelter.
  13. measure / journey – we will measure a long journey across the state.
  14. Example: I always show respect by listening when others speak.
  15. Example: Our family took a fun journey to the Grand Canyon last summer.
