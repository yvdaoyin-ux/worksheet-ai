Answer Key

  1. 96 ÷ 4 = 24 (4 goes into 9 two times, remainder 1; bring down 6 → 16 ÷ 4 = 4)
  2. 527 ÷ 5 = 105 remainder 2 (5 into 52 goes 10 times, remainder 2; bring down 7 → 27 ÷ 5 = 5, remainder 2)
  3. 1,236 ÷ 12 = 103 (12 into 12 =1, bring down 3 → 12 into 3 =0, bring down 6 → 12 into 36 =3)
  4. 1,284 ÷ 6 = 214 remainder 0 (6 goes into 12 =2, into 8 =1 remainder 2, bring down 4 → 24 ÷ 6 =4; no remainder)
  5. 4,560 ÷ 8 = 570 (8 into 45 =5 remainder 5; bring down 6 → 56 ÷ 8 =7; bring down 0 → 0 ÷ 8 =0)
  6. 7,845 ÷ 15 = 523 remainder 0 (15 into 78 =5 remainder 3; bring down 4 → 34 ÷ 15 =2 remainder 4; bring down 5 → 45 ÷ 15 =3)
  7. 84 ÷ 7 = 12 (The number line shows 12 equal jumps of 7 from 0 to 84)
  8. Each row contains 6 objects (The array has 4 rows and 6 columns, so 24 ÷ 4 = 6)
  9. 7.2 ÷ 3 = 2.4 (3 goes into 7 → 2 remainder 1; bring down .2 → 12 ÷ 3 = 4, place decimal)
  10. 452 ÷ 9 = 50 remainder 2 (9 into 45 =5, remainder 0; bring down 2 → 2 ÷ 9 =0 remainder 2. So 50 full packs, 2 stickers left)
