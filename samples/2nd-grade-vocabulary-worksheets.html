Answer Key

  1. brave – matches the definition of feeling not scared.
  2. giggle – matches the definition of a small, happy laugh.
  3. whisper – matches the definition of speaking very softly.
  4. mountain – matches the definition of a very tall hill.
  5. garden – matches the definition of a place where plants grow.
  6. shiny – matches the definition of bright and reflecting light.
  7. journey – matches the definition of traveling from one place to another.
  8. friend – matches the definition of someone you like to play with.
  9. brave – the brave climber reached the top.
  10. whisper – a quiet whisper is heard in the library.
  11. garden – the garden needs water.
  12. friend – my friend shared a sandwich.
  13. shiny – the pond was shiny and reflected the sky.
  14. Any correct sentence that uses the word “giggle,” e.g., “I hear a giggle from my brother.”
  15. Any correct sentence that uses the word “journey,” e.g., “Our family went on a journey to the beach.”
