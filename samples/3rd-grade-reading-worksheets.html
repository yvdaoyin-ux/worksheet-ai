A Day at the Town Library

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

Sarah and her best friend, Miguel, visited the town library for the first time on a bright Saturday morning. They walked past the green fence, climbed the stone steps, and entered a quiet room full of shelves that reached the ceiling. The librarian, Ms. Patel, greeted them with a warm smile and showed them a map of the library. Sarah chose a book about space rockets, while Miguel picked a story about a brave squirrel that lives in the park. After reading, they sat on the small chairs by the window, drank lemonade, and talked about the adventure they would have once they finished the books.

  1. What were the names of the two children?
  2. On what day did they visit the library?
  3. What did Miguel choose to read?
  4. What was the main idea of the passage?
  5. Why did the librarian show them a map?
  6. How do you know the library had shelves that reached the ceiling?
  7. Why did Sarah and Miguel sit by the window?
  8. What might happen after they finish the books?
  9. What does the librarian's smile suggest about her feelings?
  10. How do you know Sarah chose a book about space rockets?

Answer Key

  1. Sarah and Miguel – the passage says “Sarah and her best friend, Miguel.”
  2. Saturday morning – the passage mentions “bright Saturday morning.”
  3. A story about a brave squirrel that lives in the park – the passage says “Miguel picked a story about a brave squirrel that lives in the park.”
  4. The main idea is that the children visited the library, chose books, read, and planned an adventure afterward – this captures the overall purpose of the paragraph.
  5. She showed them a map of the library – the passage says “Ms. Patel greeted them with a warm smile and showed them a map of the library.”
  6. “the quiet room full of shelves that reached the ceiling.” – this sentence directly states the shelves reach the ceiling.
  7. They sat by the window to drink lemonade and talk – the passage says “sat on the small chairs by the window, drank lemonade, and talked….”
  8. After finishing the books, they would go on the adventure they discussed – the passage ends with “talked about the adventure they would have once they finished the books.”
  9. The librarian’s warm smile suggests friendliness and a welcoming attitude – “greeted them with a warm smile.”
  10. “Sarah chose a book about space rockets.” – the passage explicitly states this sentence.
