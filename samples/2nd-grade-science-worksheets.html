How Plants Grow

Plants need three things to grow: sunlight, water, and soil. Sunlight gives the plant energy to make food. Water moves the food through the plant’s leaves, stems, and roots. Soil holds the plant upright and gives it nutrients. When a seed gets sunlight, water, and soil, it sprouts a tiny root and a shoot. The shoot grows leaves that catch more sunlight. As the plant gets bigger, it makes more food, grows taller, and can bloom flowers or bear fruit. All living things, including people, depend on plants for food, oxygen, and shelter.

Answer Key

  1. Sunlight, water, and soil – these are the three basic needs.
  2. Sunlight gives the plant energy to make food.
  3. Water moves the food through the plant’s leaves, stems, and roots.
  4. Soil holds the roots, keeping the plant upright and giving nutrients.
  5. A tiny root and a shoot (stem) appear first.
  6. Leaves catch sunlight so the plant can make more food.
  7. Plants use sunlight to make food, and water moves the food through the plant.
  8. It can bloom flowers or grow fruit.
  9. People use plants for food.
  10. Without water the plant cannot move food, so it does not grow well.
