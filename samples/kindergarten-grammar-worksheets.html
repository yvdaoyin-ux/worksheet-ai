Grammar: Capitalizing Proper Nouns

Rewrite each sentence correctly.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. my friend alice went to the park.
  2. we visited new york city last summer.
  3. the school is named lincoln elementary.
  4. on monday we read a book.
  5. my birthday is in july.
  6. i saw a squirrel in central park.
  7. our teacher mr. smith likes cats.
  8. the flag of united states is red, white, and blue.
  9. we celebrate thanksgiving in november.
  10. my family lives in texas.

Answer Key

  1. My friend Alice went to the park. (Capitalize the proper name “Alice.”)
  2. We visited New York City last summer. (Capitalize the proper place name “New York City.”)
  3. The school is named Lincoln Elementary. (Capitalize the proper name “Lincoln Elementary.”)
  4. On Monday we read a book. (Capitalize the day of the week “Monday.”)
  5. My birthday is in July. (Capitalize the month “July.”)
  6. I saw a squirrel in Central Park. (Capitalize the proper place name “Central Park.”)
  7. Our teacher Mr. Smith likes cats. (Capitalize the title and name “Mr. Smith.”)
  8. The flag of the United States is red, white, and blue. (Capitalize the country name “United States.”)
  9. We celebrate Thanksgiving in November. (Capitalize the holiday “Thanksgiving” and the month “November.”)
  10. My family lives in Texas. (Capitalize the state name “Texas.”)
