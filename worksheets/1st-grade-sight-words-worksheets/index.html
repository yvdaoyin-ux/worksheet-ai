The Curious Cat

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

The cat sat on the mat. It was a sunny day. The cat looked at a small red ball. It jumped, then rolled. The ball rolled under the door. The cat saw a bird outside. The cat yawned. It decided to sleep.

  1. What did the cat do on the mat?
  2. What color was the ball?
  3. Under what does the ball roll?
  4. What did the cat see outside?
  5. What did the cat do after yawning?
  6. What is the main idea of the passage?
  7. How do you know the cat was sleepy? (quote the sentence)
  8. Why did the cat look at the ball?
  9. Did the cat jump or roll first?
  10. Is the cat inside the house or outside?
