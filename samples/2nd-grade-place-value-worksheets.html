Place Value Practice

Solve each problem. Show your work if needed.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. Write the number that has 4 hundreds, 3 tens, and 2 ones. ________
  2. What is the value of the digit 7 in 675? ________
  3. 300 + 50 + 8 = ________
  4. Compare: 456 \_\_\_\_ 465 (Write <, >, or =)
  5. Skip count by 100s: 200, 300, \_\_\_\_, 500
  6. Write 614 in expanded form. ________ + ________ + ________
  7. Mia has 345 stickers. She gives 128 stickers to her friend. How many stickers does Mia have now?
  8. A school has 237 students in the morning and 182 students in the afternoon. How many students are there in all?
  9. Look at the place value blocks. What number is shown?
    ________
  10. Look at the array of dots.
    How many dots are there? ________
    Is that number even or odd? ________

Answer Key

  1. 432 (4 hundreds = 400, 3 tens = 30, 2 ones = 2; 400+30+2=432).
  2. 70 (The 7 is in the tens place, so its value is 7 tens = 70).
  3. 358 (300 + 50 = 350, 350 + 8 = 358).
  4. < (456 is less than 465 because 56 < 65, or compare hundreds: same, tens: 5 vs 6, so 456 < 465).
  5. 400 (Skip counting by 100: 200, 300, 400, 500).
  6. 600 + 10 + 4 (614 = 6 hundreds + 1 ten + 4 ones).
  7. 217 (Subtract: 345 - 128. Regroup: 3 hundreds 4 tens 5 ones minus 1 hundred 2 tens 8 ones. 5-8 needs regrouping: borrow 1 ten → 15-8=7; 3 tens left, 3-2=1; 3-1=2 → 217).
  8. 419 (Add: 237 + 182. 7+2=9; 3+8=11, write 1 carry 1; 2+1+1=4 → 419).
  9. 509 (The place value blocks show 5 hundreds, 0 tens, 9 ones = 509).
  10. 8 dots (2 rows × 4 columns = 8). Even (because 8 can be split into two equal groups of 4).
