Grammar: Verb‑Tense Consistency

Rewrite each sentence correctly, keeping the verb tenses consistent.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. Yesterday, I will go to the park and played soccer.
  2. She reads a book every night and watched a movie last weekend.
  3. They are eating breakfast when the phone rang.
  4. My brother will finish his homework and then he ate dinner.
  5. The dog chased the cat and then it is sleeping.
  6. I am going to the store tomorrow and bought milk.
  7. We have seen that movie and we liked it.
  8. He will write a letter and mailed it yesterday.
  9. The class will start at 9 AM and we started early.
  10. She is drawing a picture and she drew a tree yesterday.

Answer Key

  1. Yesterday, I went to the park and played soccer. (Both verbs in past tense.)
  2. She read a book every night and watched a movie last weekend. (Both verbs in past simple.)
  3. They were eating breakfast when the phone rang. (Both verbs in past progressive to match the past event.)
  4. My brother will finish his homework and then will eat dinner. (Future tense for both actions.)
  5. The dog chased the cat and then slept. (Both verbs in past tense.)
  6. I will go to the store tomorrow and will buy milk. (Future tense for both actions.)
  7. We have seen that movie and liked it. (Past perfect and simple past are both past‑time; keep past consistency.)
  8. He wrote a letter and mailed it yesterday. (Both verbs in past simple.)
  9. The class will start at 9 AM and we will start early. (Future tense for both actions.)
  10. She was drawing a picture and drew a tree yesterday. (Both verbs in past progressive/simple past for past actions.)
