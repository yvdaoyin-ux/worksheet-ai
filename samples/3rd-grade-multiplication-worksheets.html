  7. Maya has 4 packs of stickers. Each pack contains 9 stickers. How many stickers does she have in total? _____
  8. A garden has 6 rows of tomato plants. Each row has 7 plants. How many tomato plants are there? _____

  10. Use the number‑line visual to show 5 \times 6.
    After 6 jumps of size 5, what number is reached? _____

Answer Key

  1. 56 – because 7 \times 8 = 56.
  2. 54 – because 9 \times 6 = 54.
  3. 80 – because 4 \times 20 = 4 \times (2 \times 10) = 80.
  4. 180 – because 6 \times 30 = 6 \times (3 \times 10) = 180.
  5. 0 – any number multiplied by 0 equals 0.
  6. 80 – because 8 \times 10 = 80.
  7. 36 – 4 packs × 9 stickers per pack = 4 \times 9 = 36 stickers.
  8. 42 – 6 rows × 7 plants per row = 6 \times 7 = 42 plants.
  9. 12 – the array shows 3 rows of 4 dots, so 3 \times 4 = 12.
  10. 30 – starting at 0, 6 jumps of 5 each land on 30 (5 \times 6 = 30).
