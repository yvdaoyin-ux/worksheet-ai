Grammar: Capitalizing Proper Nouns

Rewrite each sentence correctly.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. i went to the park with my friend john.
  2. the school is located in new york city.
  3. monday is my favorite day of the week.
  4. our teacher, mr. smith, gave us a test.
  5. i read the book 'the hobbit' yesterday.
  6. we went to the statue of liberty in new york.
  7. i have a dog named max.
  8. the president of the united states lives in the white house.
  9. our class will visit the smithsonian museum next week.
  10. tom is celebrating his birthday on april 22.

Answer Key

  1. I went to the park with my friend John. (Capitalized I, John, proper nouns.)
  2. The school is located in New York City. (Capitalized New, York, City.)
  3. Monday is my favorite day of the week. (Capitalized Monday.)
  4. Our teacher, Mr. Smith, gave us a test. (Capitalized Our, Mr., Smith.)
  5. I read the book 'The Hobbit' yesterday. (Capitalized I, The, Hobbit.)
  6. We went to the Statue of Liberty in New York. (Capitalized Statue, Liberty, New, York.)
  7. I have a dog named Max. (Capitalized I, Max.)
  8. The President of the United States lives in the White House. (Capitalized President, United, States, White, House.)
  9. Our class will visit the Smithsonian museum next week. (Capitalized Our, Smithsonian.)
  10. Tom is celebrating his birthday on April 22. (Capitalized Tom, April.)
