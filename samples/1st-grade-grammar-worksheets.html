Grammar: Capitalizing Proper Nouns

Rewrite each sentence correctly.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. my brother went to the zoo with alice.
  2. we visited the statue of liberty on our field trip.
  3. my favorite book is about harry potter.
  4. yesterday we ate lunch at mcdonalds.
  5. the teacher said, "please hand in your work to mr. smith."
  6. my family lives in california.
  7. on july 4th we watched fireworks.
  8. my best friend is named emily.
  9. we went to the grand canyon for vacation.
  10. my cat's name is whiskers.

Answer Key

  1. My brother went to the zoo with Alice. (Capitalize names of people.)
  2. We visited the Statue of Liberty on our field trip. (Capitalize names of places and landmarks.)
  3. My favorite book is about Harry Potter. (Capitalize titles of characters.)
  4. Yesterday we ate lunch at McDonald's. (Capitalize brand names, include apostrophe.)
  5. The teacher said, "please hand in your work to Mr. Smith." (Capitalize titles and surnames.)
  6. My family lives in California. (Capitalize state names.)
  7. On July 4th we watched fireworks. (Capitalize month names.)
  8. My best friend is named Emily. (Capitalize personal names.)
  9. We went to the Grand Canyon for vacation. (Capitalize geographic names.)
  10. My cat's name is Whiskers. (Capitalize proper names for pets.)
