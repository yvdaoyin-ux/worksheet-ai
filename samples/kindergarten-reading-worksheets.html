  1. What animal does Lily see at the zoo?
  2. What is the giraffe eating?
  3. Who is Lily walking with?
  4. Why does Lily wave at the giraffe?

  1. The giraffe.
  2. Leaves.
  3. Her mom.
  4. Because she wants to greet it; the passage says, “Lily waves at the giraffe and says, ‘Hello, giraffe!’”
  5. Happy.
  6. The zoo.
