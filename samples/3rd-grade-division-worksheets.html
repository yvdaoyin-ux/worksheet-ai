Answer Key

  1. 8 – 6 fits into 48 eight times.
  2. 9 – 9 fits into 81 nine times.
  3. 8 – 7 fits into 56 eight times.
  4. 21 – 3 fits into 63 twenty‑one times.
  5. 18 – 5 fits into 90 eighteen times.
  6. 9 – 3 fits into 27 nine times.
  7. 8 – 32 ÷ 4 = 8.
  8. 5 – 45 ÷ 9 = 5.
  9. 6 – 30 ÷ 5 = 6 jumps.
  10. 4 – 20 ÷ 5 = 4 objects per row.
