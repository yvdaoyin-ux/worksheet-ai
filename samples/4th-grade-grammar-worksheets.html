Grammar: Verb Tense Consistency

Rewrite each sentence correctly.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. Yesterday, Sarah go to the park.
  2. My brother and I plays soccer every Sunday.
  3. Tomorrow we will be watching a movie.
  4. She reads a book, then she read a poem.
  5. The students were studying when the bell rang, and then they will go home.
  6. I am going to the store, and I bought milk.
  7. She will dance, but she dance now.
  8. They have finished the project and they is ready to submit.
  9. The dog chased the cat and then it chase the mailman.
  10. If I have enough time, I will study, but I studies too late.

Answer Key

  1. Yesterday, Sarah went to the park. (Past tense consistency)
  2. My brother and I play soccer every Sunday. (Present tense consistency)
  3. Tomorrow we will watch a movie. (Future tense consistency)
  4. She reads a book, then she reads a poem. (Present tense consistency)
  5. The students were studying when the bell rang, and then they went home. (Past tense consistency)
  6. I went to the store, and I bought milk. (Past tense consistency)
  7. She will dance, but she dances now. (Present vs. Future consistency)
  8. They have finished the project and they are ready to submit. (Present tense consistency)
  9. The dog chased the cat and then it chased the mailman. (Past tense consistency)
  10. If I have enough time, I will study, but I study too late. (Present vs. Future consistency)
