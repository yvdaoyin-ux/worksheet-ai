Answer Key

  1. 75
  2. 56
  3. Digit 4 is in the tens place, so its value is 4 × 10 = 40.
  4. Counting by 5s: 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 → 7th number = 30.
  5. 625
  6. 637
  7. 3 quarters = 75¢, 2 dimes = 20¢, 4 pennies = 4¢; total = 99¢ = $0.99.
  8. $10 – $7 = $3 change.
  9. The place‑value visual shows 4‑8‑3, so the number is 483.
  10. 276 + 149 = 425 (add column by column).
