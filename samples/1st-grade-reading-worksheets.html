Sam's Fun Day at the Park

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

Sam and his dog, Bella, went to the park on Saturday. They saw a red kite flying high. Sam laughed and ran after the kite. Bella barked and chased a butterfly. After playing, they ate a sandwich on a bench.

  1. What day did Sam and Bella go to the park?
  2. What animal did Sam bring with him?
  3. What color was the kite?
  4. What did Sam do when he saw the kite?
  5. What did Bella do while Sam ran?
  6. What did they eat at the end of their play?
  7. Where did they eat their sandwich?
  8. What is the main idea of the passage?
  9. How do you know that Sam and Bella had fun?
  10. Who was the story about?

Answer Key

  1. They went to the park on Saturday. (The passage says “on Saturday.”)
  2. Sam brought his dog, Bella. (The first sentence mentions “his dog, Bella.”)
  3. The kite was red. (The passage describes “a red kite.”)
  4. Sam laughed and ran after the kite. (It states “Sam laughed and ran after the kite.”)
  5. Bella barked and chased a butterfly. (The text says “Bella barked and chased a butterfly.”)
  6. They ate a sandwich. (The passage notes “they ate a sandwich.”)
  7. They ate the sandwich on a bench. (It says “on a bench.”)
  8. The main idea is that Sam and Bella went to the park. (All sentences talk about Sam and Bella at the park.)
  9. Because the passage says Sam laughed and ran after the kite and Bella barked and chased a butterfly. (These actions are described in the story.)
  10. The story was about Sam and his dog Bella. (The passage focuses on Sam and Bella’s actions.)
