Halves, Thirds, and Fourths

Write the fraction for each shaded part. Then answer the questions.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. What fraction of the circle is shaded?
  2. What fraction of the rectangle is shaded?
  3. What fraction of the circle is shaded?
  4. A sandwich is cut into 2 equal parts. What is each part called?
  5. A pizza is cut into 4 equal slices. Maya eats 1 slice. What fraction of the pizza does Maya eat?
  6. Ben has a cake cut into 3 equal pieces. He gives 1 piece to his sister. What fraction of the cake is left?
  7. Write the fraction for one half.
  8. Write the fraction for three fourths.
  9. A shape is divided into 3 equal parts. What fraction names one part?
  10. What fraction of the rectangle is shaded?

Answer Key

  1. \frac{1}{4} — The circle has 4 equal parts, and 1 part is shaded.
  2. \frac{3}{4} — The rectangle has 4 equal parts, and 3 parts are shaded.
  3. \frac{1}{2} — The circle has 2 equal parts, and 1 part is shaded.
  4. A half (\frac{1}{2}) — Cutting something into 2 equal parts makes halves.
  5. \frac{1}{4} — The pizza has 4 equal slices, and Maya eats 1 slice.
  6. \frac{2}{3} — The cake has 3 equal pieces. After giving away 1 piece, 2 pieces are left.
  7. \frac{1}{2} — One half is 1 part out of 2 equal parts.
  8. \frac{3}{4} — Three fourths is 3 parts out of 4 equal parts.
  9. \frac{1}{3} — The shape has 3 equal parts, so 1 part is one third.
  10. \frac{1}{3} — The rectangle has 3 equal parts, and 1 part is shaded.
