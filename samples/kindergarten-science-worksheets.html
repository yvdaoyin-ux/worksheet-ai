All About Plants

Plants are living things. They need sunlight, water, air, and soil to grow. Roots drink water from the soil and hold the plant in place. Leaves catch sunlight and use it to make food in a process called photosynthesis. Seeds can grow into new plants when they are planted in soil, get water, and get sunlight. Some plants have flowers that make seeds. Animals, including people, eat plants for energy. In the spring, many plants grow new leaves and buds. In the fall, leaves may change color and fall to the ground. Taking care of plants helps keep our world healthy.

  2. Where do roots get water?
  3. Which part of the plant catches sunlight?

  5. What three things help a seed become a new plant?
  6. Why do animals eat plants?

Answer Key

  1. Sunlight, water, air, and soil – these are the basic things plants need.
  2. From the soil – roots soak up water that’s in the ground.
  3. Leaves – they are the flat parts that catch the sun’s light.
  4. Photosynthesis – the name of the food‑making process.
  5. Soil, water, and sunlight – all three give a seed the conditions to grow.
  6. Animals need energy, and plants provide that energy.
  7. Leaves change color and fall to the ground – that is a typical fall change.
  8. Taking care of plants keeps our world healthy.
  9. Roots hold the plant down so it doesn’t tip over.
  10. Flowers make seeds, which let new plants grow.
