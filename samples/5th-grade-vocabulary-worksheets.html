Vocabulary: 5th Grade Words

Use the word bank to complete each activity.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

adapt ecosystem persuade ancient fraction reliable consequence visible

A. Match each word to its meaning. B. Fill in the blank. C. Write your own sentence for two words.

  1. To change in order to fit new conditions.
  2. A community of living things interacting with their environment.
  3. To convince someone to do or believe something.
  4. Very old; from a long time ago.
  5. A part of a whole expressed with a numerator and denominator.
  6. Someone or something you can count on.
  7. A result or effect of an action.
  8. Can be seen; not hidden.
  9. Animals must __________ to survive when their habitat changes.
  10. The Amazon rainforest is an example of a diverse __________.
  11. She tried to __________ her friend to join the club.
  12. The pyramids are __________ structures built thousands of years ago.
  13. He solved the problem by writing the answer as a __________.
  14. My watch is __________; it never misses a minute.
  15. Skipping class can have a serious __________.
  16. Write a sentence using the word "adapt".
  17. Write a sentence using the word "consequence".

Answer Key

  1. adapt – the definition describes changing to fit new conditions.
  2. ecosystem – matches the definition of a community of living things and their environment.
  3. persuade – means to convince someone.
  4. ancient – means very old.
  5. fraction – a part of a whole expressed with numerator/denominator.
  6. reliable – describes something you can count on.
  7. consequence – the result or effect of an action.
  8. visible – something that can be seen.
  9. adapt
  10. ecosystem
  11. persuade
  12. ancient
  13. fraction
  14. reliable
  15. consequence
  16. Example: “The chameleon can adapt its color to blend in with its surroundings.” (Any correct sentence that uses “adapt” earns full credit.)
  17. Example: “The consequence of forgetting to do homework was losing recess time.” (Any correct sentence that uses “consequence” earns full credit.)
