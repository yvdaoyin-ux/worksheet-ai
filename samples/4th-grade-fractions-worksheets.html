Answer each question. Show your work for the computation problems.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

  1. \frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = _____
  2. 7 \times 8 = _____
  3. 125 \div 5 = _____
  4. 2\frac{1}{3} + 1\frac{2}{3} = _____
  5. 5 \times \frac{2}{5} = _____
  6. 9 \times 12 = _____
  7. Pizza was cut into 8 equal slices. Sarah ate 3 slices and Tom ate 1 slice. What fraction of the pizza is left? _____
  8. A recipe calls for 2\frac{1}{2} cups of flour. If you double the recipe, how many cups of flour do you need? _____
  9. Using the number line, compare \frac{3}{8} and \frac{1}{2}. Which fraction is larger?
  10. Shade the fraction \frac{2}{4} on the bar. Then write an equivalent fraction with denominator 8.

Answer Key

  1. 1 — same denominator, add numerators: 3+1=4, so 4/4 = 1.
  2. 56 — 7 times 8.
  3. 25 — 125 divided by 5.
  4. 4 — convert to improper fractions or add whole parts and fractions: 2 1/3 + 1 2/3 = 3 (1/3+2/3)=3 1 = 4.
  5. 2 — multiply: (5 × 2)/5 = 10/5 = 2.
  6. 108 — 9 times 12.
  7. \frac{1}{2} — 8 slices total, 4 eaten, 4 left → 4/8 = 1/2.
  8. 5 cups — 2 1/2 × 2 = 5.
  9. \frac{1}{2} — on the number line, \frac{1}{2} (4/8) is to the right of \frac{3}{8}.
  10. \frac{4}{8} (which is also \frac{1}{2}) — multiply numerator and denominator of \frac{2}{4} by 2.
