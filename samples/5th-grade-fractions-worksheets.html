Answer Key

  1. 5\frac{9}{20}. Convert to common denominator 20, add: 65/20 + 44/20 = 109/20 = 5\frac{9}{20}.
  2. 5\frac{19}{24}. Common denominator 24, subtract: 171/24 - 32/24 = 139/24 = 5\frac{19}{24}.
  3. 3\frac{1}{3}. Multiply: 5 × 2/3 = 10/3 = 3\frac{1}{3}.
  4. \frac{3}{8}. Divide by 2 = multiply by 1/2: (3/4) × (1/2) = 3/8.
  5. 4.5. Multiply: 12 × 0.375 = 4.5.
  6. 29. Divide: 145 ÷ 5 = 29.
  7. 1\frac{3}{4} cups. Subtract: 3\frac{1}{2} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 7/2 - 7/4 = 7/4 = 1\frac{3}{4}.
  8. 30 cubic feet. Volume = 4 × 3 × 2.5 = 30.
  9. 0.375. Convert \frac{3}{8} to decimal: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.
  10. 385. Add columnwise: 236 + 149 = 385.
