  15. Write a sentence using “blue”.

Answer Key

  1. cat – it’s the animal that says “meow”.
  2. ball – a round toy for throwing or kicking.
  3. happy – the word for feeling smiley.
  4. big – means larger than most things.
  5. blue – the sky’s color.
  6. run – to move fast on your feet.
  7. tree – a tall plant with leaves.
  8. eat – to put food in your mouth.
