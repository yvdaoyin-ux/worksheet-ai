Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

WorksheetAI helps homeschool parents, K–5 teachers and tutors create printable worksheets. This page explains what data we handle.

What we collect

You don't need an account. We don't collect personal information to use the generator. Your grade/subject/topic choices and your “unlocked” status are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — they are not sent to us.

Payments

Payments are handled by Gumroad. We never see or store your card details. Please see Gumroad's privacy policy for how they process payment information.

AI generation

When you generate a worksheet, the topic text you type is sent to a third-party AI provider solely to produce the worksheet content. Please don't enter personal or sensitive information in the topic field.

Cookies / analytics

We do not use advertising cookies. Any local storage is limited to the settings above.

Contact

For questions or refund requests, contact us through the Gumroad product page.
