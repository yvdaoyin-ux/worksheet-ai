A Sunny Day in the Community Garden

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

On a sunny Saturday morning, Maya and her older brother, Alex, headed to the community garden behind the library. They carried a small backpack filled with gardening gloves, a trowel, and packets of sunflower seeds. The garden was a patchwork of raised beds, each bursting with tomatoes, lettuce, and bright marigolds that chased away bugs.

Maya’s favorite part was the row of tall sunflowers that swayed like golden flags. When they arrived, the garden volunteer, Mrs. Patel, greeted them with a warm smile. She showed them how to dig a shallow hole, place three seeds about an inch apart, and cover them gently with soil. Maya pressed each seed into the earth, feeling the cool dirt between her fingers.

Over the next few weeks, Maya checked the seedlings every afternoon. She watered them carefully, pulled weeds, and watched the tiny green shoots push toward the sky. By late August, the sunflowers stood taller than Maya, their heads drooping with heavy, dark seeds ready for harvest.

  2. What items did Maya and Alex bring to the garden?
  3. Describe the role of Mrs. Patel in the garden.
  4. What steps did Maya follow to plant the sunflower seeds?
  5. How did Maya care for the seedlings after planting?
  6. What is the main idea of the passage?
  7. Why do you think the garden volunteers use marigolds in the beds?
  8. How does the author show Maya’s feelings about gardening?
  9. By the end of the passage, how have the sunflowers changed?
  10. What lesson about responsibility can a reader learn from Maya’s experience?

Answer Key

  1. Maya and her older brother, Alex, are the main characters.
  2. They brought gardening gloves, a trowel, and packets of sunflower seeds (carried in a small backpack).
  3. Mrs. Patel is the garden volunteer who greets the children and shows them how to plant the seeds.
  4. She dug a shallow hole, placed three seeds about an inch apart, covered them gently with soil, and pressed each seed into the earth.
  5. She watered the seedlings carefully, pulled weeds, and watched the shoots grow each afternoon.
  6. The passage’s main idea is that Maya learns how to plant and care for sunflowers in a community garden, showing growth and responsibility.
  7. Marigolds are used because, as the passage states, they “chased away bugs,” helping protect the other plants.
  8. The author shows Maya’s feelings by noting “Maya’s favorite part was the row of tall sunflowers…” and that she “pressed each seed into the earth, feeling the cool dirt between her fingers.”
  9. By late August the sunflowers “stood taller than Maya, their heads drooping with heavy, dark seeds ready for harvest.”
  10. The story teaches that consistent care and responsibility lead to growth and a rewarding harvest.
