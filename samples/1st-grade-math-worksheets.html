Answer Key

  2. 8 – subtract the ones.
  3. 17 – add the ones.
  4. 7 – subtract the ones.
  5. 52 – add 7 to the ones place of 45.
  6. 9 – 0 plus 9 is 9.
  7. 10 – 6 + 4 = 10.
  8. 8 – subtract 5 from 13
  9. 31 – it is to the right of 23 on the number line.
