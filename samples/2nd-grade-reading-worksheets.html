Helping a Lost Dog

Read the passage. Then answer the questions in complete sentences.

Name: ____________Date: ____________Score: ______

Ethan loves to play at Maple Park on Saturday afternoons. One sunny day he was building a sandcastle near the playground when he heard a faint bark. A small, brown dog was lying on the grass beside the bench. Ethan walked over and saw a tag on its collar. The tag said “Buddy” and had a phone number. He called the number and the voice on the phone was his grandma’s. The grandma told Ethan that Buddy had gone missing while she was at the grocery store. They all went back to Maple Park, and Buddy happily ran to his grandma’s arms.

  1. Who was playing at Maple Park?
  2. What did Ethan hear?
  3. Where was the dog lying?
  4. What did the dog’s collar say?
  5. What did Ethan do after seeing the tag?
  6. Who answered the phone when Ethan called?
  7. Why did the grandma call Ethan back? (How do you know)
  8. What is the main idea of the passage?
  9. How do you know that Buddy is the dog’s name? (How do you know)
  10. Why is it good that Ethan helped the dog?

Answer Key

  1. Ethan. (The first sentence names him.)
  2. He heard a faint bark. (Sentence 2 describes the sound.)
  3. On the grass beside the bench. (Sentence 3 tells the location.)
  4. “Buddy”. (Sentence 4 states the tag text.)
  5. He called the phone number on the tag. (Sentence 5 shows his action.)
  6. His grandma. (Sentence 6 identifies the caller.)
  7. Because Buddy had gone missing while she was at the grocery store. (The sentence in parentheses shows this.)
  8. Ethan helps a lost dog find its owner. (Main idea inferred from the entire story.)
  9. Because the tag said “Buddy”. (Direct quote from the passage.)
  10. It brought Buddy back safely and made everyone happy. (Inference from the joyful ending.)
